Clinical Procedure: Central Venous Catheter Insertion

Purpose

This clinical procedure describes the standard steps for central venous catheter insertion and post-procedure monitoring.

Indications

  • Long-term IV access
  • Hemodynamic monitoring
  • Administration of vesicant medications

Pre-Procedure Checklist

Confirm informed consent, verify site with ultrasound, and complete the central line insertion checklist per protocol.

Procedure Steps

  • Prepare sterile field and maximal barrier precautions
  • Identify vessel via ultrasound guidance
  • Insert catheter using Seldinger technique
  • Confirm placement via chest x-ray

Post-Procedure Monitoring

Monitor insertion site for signs of infection every shift. Document dressing changes per protocol.
