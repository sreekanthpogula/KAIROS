Regulatory Update: CMS Reimbursement and Compliance Requirements

Overview

This regulatory document summarizes updated CMS requirements affecting provider reimbursement and compliance reporting for the upcoming plan year.

Effective Date

The updated regulation and associated compliance requirements take effect January 1, 2027.

Key Requirements

  • Updated CPT code reporting requirements
  • Revised reimbursement rate disclosure obligations
  • New provider directory accuracy attestation

Compliance Deadlines

Organizations must complete the provider directory attestation by November 1, 2026, in accordance with the regulatory guidance.

Applicability

This regulation applies to all providers and payers participating in Medicare Advantage and Medicaid managed care programs.
